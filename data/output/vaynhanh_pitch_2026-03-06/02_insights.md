### Slide 8: SWOT
**Strengths:** Asset-light (zero NPL), 30M+ users, 64% reloan rate, 1-min approval
**Weaknesses:** VietCredit 66% concentration, declining approval rate (74%→69%), ticket cap 18M
**Opportunities:** MBV diversification (80% approval), BNPL $8.5B market, loan limit 4x increase, 1.59M cross-sell pool
**Threats:** SeaMoney off-platform +300%, Cake 6.2M users, VietCredit credit card independence

### Slide 9: Strategy 2026-2027

**Pillar 1: Lender Diversification**
- Target: 6+ lenders, max 40% concentration
- Priority: Onboard 2 more bank partners (after MBV success: 80% approval)
- Unlock higher tickets: 25-30M avg by 2027

**Pillar 2: Product Expansion**
- Secured lending (asset-backed, higher amounts)
- BNPL integration with MoMo merchant ecosystem
- Risk-based pricing: premium rates for higher-risk, lower for prime

**Pillar 3: AI Credit Scoring**
- Leverage 60+ domain data (payments, TTT, insurtech) for proprietary scoring
- Goal: Improve approval rate back to 75%+ while maintaining NPL
- Sell scoring-as-a-service to lender partners

**Pillar 4: Cross-sell Engine**
- 1.59M VietQR users with no FS products → Vay Nhanh leads
- 995K dormant Paylater users → reactivation via Vay Nhanh
- Paylater graduates → higher-ticket Vay Nhanh
